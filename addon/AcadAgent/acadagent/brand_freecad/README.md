# The FreeCAD logo (in the app)

Byte-for-byte copies of `docs/brand/freecad/` (the FreeCAD wordmark, from the
FreeCAD Project Association's official logo kit). The app shows it as
"Based on FreeCAD", linked to https://www.freecad.org: in the sidebar, on
Home and in the top bar (`shell.FreeCadBadge`, `style.freecad_wordmark`).

The FreeCAD logo is a trademark of the FreeCAD Project Association AISBL
(FPA). It is used only to say that Atech Atelier is built on FreeCAD, as the
FPA's brand guidelines and https://wiki.freecad.org/License allow. It is not
Atech's logo and does not mean the FreeCAD project or the FPA endorses Atech
AI CAD. Do not alter these files.
